The Iliad

We begin with the Iliad because it is an enduring expression of the ethos of the warrior clan.

Nevertheless, we begin to see cracks appearing in the coherent world of the warrior clan: Achilles fails to fulfill his role in the clan structure. Homer is writing about this world hundreds of years after the events he describes, and he is already beginning to see the limitations of the warrior ethos.
